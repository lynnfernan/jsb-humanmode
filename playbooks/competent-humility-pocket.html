The Competent Humility Playbook

Pocket card · Five practices · Companion to the Competent Humility Pulse Check

Human Mode

Own your craft. Name the complexity. Leave oxygen for the unknown — then decide.

1 · Know / Don’t know / DecideHere’s what I know. Here’s what no one can really know at this point. Here’s how we’ll decide — and by when.
2 · Pre-mortem (you go first)“It’s six months from now. This failed. What did we miss?” One real risk from you. Top three. Owners.

3 · Claim craft (Quiet Experts)

“Here’s where I’m confident — [domain]. Here’s where I want your pressure-test — [domain].”

4 · Dissenting junior (Certainty Actors)

“What are we not seeing?” Protect the answer. Thank them in the room.

5 · Repair within 48 hoursIn that meeting I overstated how settled this is. What’s actually still open is [X]. Here’s what I need from you now — [specific].
Traps: Certainty Actor · Quiet Expert · Open Amateur → target: Competent Humility Leader
Close: Imperfection isn’t the opposite of leadership. Unowned imperfection is.
